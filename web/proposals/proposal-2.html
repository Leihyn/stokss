Volatility does not
sleep when the
market does.

The intuition is that a tokenized share goes quiet when its underlying does. It does the opposite. Hourly realised volatility is higher in the hours nobody can price than in the hours everybody can — which is exactly the window in which the reference feed is frozen and the issuer will not create or redeem.

That is the case for warning a liquidity provider before the bell rather than after it.
